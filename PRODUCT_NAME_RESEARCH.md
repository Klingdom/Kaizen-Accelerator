## 4. Naming Tactics That Work in This Category

This product sits in a specific intersection: deterministic scheduling OS + structured continuous improvement. No competitor has named this intersection well because no competitor has built it.

**Pattern ranking for this product:**

1. Short + abstract (Linear, Notion model) — highest ceiling. Implies sophistication. Easiest to defend. Hardest to explain without marketing investment. Right fit for a product whose differentiation is invisible until you use it (the 4-2-2 invariant, the INFEASIBLE state, the Kaizen close gate).

2. Evocative + concrete (Sunsama, Motion model) — middle path. Memorable. Implies experience quality. Slightly easier to explain. Risk: anchors brand to a feeling rather than a behavior, and this product's bet is on behavior change.

3. Metaphor / outcome (Reclaim model) — works if the metaphor matches the core JTBD. "Reclaim" maps directly to "protect your time." The analogous concept here might be "rhythm," "cadence," "pattern," "form," or "proof." These metaphors are closer to the product's core claim (standard work that actually runs).

4. Descriptive compound (CadencePlan, Productboard model) — lowest brand ceiling. Immediately understandable but loses verb-ability, search isolation, and defensibility. CadencePlan reads as a feature name, not a product name.

**Verdict for this product:** Pattern 1 or 3. The product's differentiation is structural and behavioral, not experiential — short + abstract or tight metaphor will age better than a descriptive compound. The 30-Day Accelerator consulting layer can have a descriptive name; the OS product should not.

## Implications for Scope and Positioning

- The product needs one external name and can keep an internal codename. BAM-X Kaizen OS is a strong internal codename; it should not be the market name.
- CadencePlan is the safe default if no new name clears trademark and domain checks in time for launch. It is not the optimal name.
- The naming pattern that fits this product's differentiation (structured, deterministic, behavioral) is short + abstract (Cadex, Vela) or tight metaphor (Cadence, Rhythm). Descriptive compounds belong on features and documentation, not on the brand.
- Whatever name is chosen, the .ai TLD is acceptable for this market and provides domain availability relief. The ICP (managers and CI practitioners at SaaS companies) has normalized .ai as a credible SaaS domain.
